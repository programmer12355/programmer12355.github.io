Arcade Shooter

This is an Arcade Shooter game. You move a spaceship and shoot descending aliens. For each alien you hit, you get 1 point, and if you miss one, you lose 5 points. You win by reaching 100 points and lose if you get to -50 points. The score is not saved.

Dev: programmer12355

Difficulty: Medium

Platform: Windows

Programming Language: C++

Status: Complete + you can write what to add to the email on the "About Me" page
