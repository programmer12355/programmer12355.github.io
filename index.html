Breakout

Click for more info

This is a modified version of the Breakout game. You move the paddle and bounce the ball. Each hit adds a point. If you miss the ball, you lose. There are no power-ups or special actions, just pure ball bouncing. The score is not saved.

Arcade Shooter

Click for more info

This is an Arcade Shooter game. You move a spaceship and shoot descending aliens. For each alien you hit, you get 1 point, and if you miss one, you lose 5 points. You win by reaching 100 points and lose if you get to -50 points. The score is not saved.
